Stacklands Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cevark Interactive ("we", "us") handles information when you use the Stacklands mobile game (the "Game").

Privacy contact

Questions, privacy requests or deletion requests: cevarkinteractive@gmail.com

1. Information the Game processes

  • Game progress and settings: progress, resources, preferences and other gameplay state may be stored locally on your device.
  • Anonymous Firebase account: the Game can use Firebase Authentication to generate a pseudonymous user identifier for services such as bug reporting. No email address or player name is required for anonymous sign-in.
  • Bug reports: when you choose to send a report, we receive the category, description and relevant technical context (such as app version, device model, operating-system version, current scene and approximate performance). If you explicitly enable the diagnostic option, the report can also contain recent warning/error logs. Do not enter passwords or other sensitive personal information in a report.
  • Advertising and consent: if advertising is enabled in your version of the Game, Google AdMob and its partners may process advertising-related information as described in their policies. Where required, the Game requests consent or presents privacy choices through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Ad functionality may not be available in every build.

2. Why we process information

We process local gameplay data to operate the Game, anonymous identifiers to support connected features, and bug-report information to investigate issues, maintain security and improve reliability. Where applicable, advertising data is processed to provide advertising and comply with users' privacy choices. The applicable legal basis depends on the activity and your jurisdiction, including provision of requested services, legitimate interests in maintaining the Game, legal obligations and consent where required.

3. Services and recipients

We use Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for anonymous sign-in and player-submitted bug reports. If advertising is enabled, we use Google AdMob and Google UMP. These services may process information according to their own policies:

  • Firebase privacy and security information
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob policies

We do not sell player-submitted bug-report text. Access to bug reports is restricted to authorized project administrators and service providers needed to operate the reporting feature.

4. Storage and retention

Local gameplay data normally remains on the device until you remove it through supported controls or remove the app and its data. Submitted reports and their technical context are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to investigate issues, maintain the service, meet applicable obligations or resolve disputes. You may request deletion of information associated with your report or anonymous account by contacting us. We may need enough information to locate the relevant record.

5. Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to submit a bug report and whether to attach optional diagnostics. Where advertising consent controls apply, you can make or change choices through the privacy options made available in the Game. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Contact us at the email above to exercise applicable rights.

6. Security and international processing

We take reasonable steps to restrict access to submitted reports and use service-provider security controls. No online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Google services may process information outside your country, subject to applicable safeguards.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is not intended to collect personal information from children through bug reports. Please do not include a child's personal details in a report. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request. Advertising behavior and age-related disclosures will depend on the final audience classification and distribution settings.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game or its services change. The revision date above will be updated. Material changes will be communicated where required.
